📌 What does this project simulate?

It models how NASA spacecraft travel across interplanetary space using Hohmann Transfer Orbits and Slingshot Gravity Assists to reach distant planets with minimum rocket fuel.

1. Hohmann Transfer Orbit

Instead of flying straight, the rocket fires its engine once at Earth to enter an elliptical arc that naturally intersects the target planet.

2. The Vis-Viva Equation

v² = GM(2/r - 1/a): Calculates exact spacecraft speed. As the probe moves further from the Sun, solar gravity continuously slows it down.

3. Vector Gravity Assist (Free Speed Boost!)

When passing near Jupiter, Jupiter's gravity bends the flight path. The probe steals a tiny fraction of Jupiter's orbital momentum, gaining a vector-calculated +9.9 km/s boost of heliocentric speed for FREE!
